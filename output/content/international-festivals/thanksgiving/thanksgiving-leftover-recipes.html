Thanksgiving Leftovers: Gourmet Transformations

Transform your Thanksgiving leftovers - turkey, stuffing, cranberry sauce, veggies, and pie - into delicious soups, sandwiches, casseroles, and other new dishes, all while adhering to safe storage guidelines

Cool and store leftovers first

In order to be creative, it is crucial to begin with proper storage. Following USDA recommendations, it is advised to refrigerate perishable leftovers within two hours of cooking or serving. To expedite the cooling process, divide large portions into shallow containers.

Refrigerate Thanksgiving leftovers and use them within four days; any unused portions should be frozen.

Turkey becomes a new protein

Utilize sliced turkey in a panini with cranberry sauce and a tangy condiment, mix chopped turkey into enchiladas, incorporate it into pot pie filling, or quickly simmer in a broth-based soup. Make sure to portion out what you need to avoid reheating the entire batch multiple times.

Stuffing, potatoes and vegetables

Stuffing can transform into crispy patties with an egg on top, a savory waffle, or the foundation of a casserole. Mashed potatoes can be made into potato cakes or used to thicken soup. Roasted vegetables are versatile and can be added to grain bowls, frittatas, or blended soups.

Desserts can become components

Break up the pie to make parfaits, blend the filling into a milkshake or yogurt swirl, or freeze for future enjoyment. This strategy cuts down on waste and transforms the day after Thanksgiving into a fun opportunity to get creative in the kitchen.

Note: Store perishable foods in the refrigerator immediately; if unsure about how to store them, it is better to throw them out than risk eating spoiled

Frequently asked questions

How long are Thanksgiving leftovers safe in the refrigerator?

According to USDA recommendations, refrigerated Thanksgiving leftovers can typically be safely consumed for up to four days if immediately cooled and stored properly.

Can I freeze Thanksgiving leftovers?

Yes, divide into meal-sized portions, label with the date, and store in freezer-safe containers for optimal freshness.
