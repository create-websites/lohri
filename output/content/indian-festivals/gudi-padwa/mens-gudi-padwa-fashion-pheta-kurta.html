Men’s Gudi Padwa Fashion: Kurta and Pheta

Achieve a stylish Maharashtrian festive attire with a comfortable combination of kurta, pajama or dhoti, Nehru jacket, and the classic pheta.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

Gudi Padwa comes alive with clothing and home décor, offering easy-to-achieve styling and festive decorations that add a touch of celebration without being overwhelming.

Start with the occasion

When planning your attire for a home puja, neighborhood procession, cultural program, or formal community event, consider the duration and level of activity for each occasion.

A well-fitted and breathable kurta can look more elegant than a heavily embroidered garment that quickly becomes uncomfortable.

Kurta colors for the festival

Saffron, cream, white, green, maroon, and gold tones complement Gudi Padwa's aesthetic, photographing beautifully alongside rangoli, marigolds, and a vibrant Gudi.

If the pheta is the focal point, opt for a simpler kurta to avoid clashing with the overall outfit.

Pajama, churidar or dhoti

Most wearers find a straight pajama or churidar easy to wear. However, a dhoti-style bottom can give a more traditional appearance, but it must be securely tied and practiced before wearing it for a long event.

Pre-sewn dhoti pants offer a convenient option for younger attendees and diaspora gatherings.

The pheta as the focal point

The pheta, a traditional Maharashtrian turban, has the ability to elevate a simple kurta into a unique festive ensemble. Saffron is a popular choice for public events and processions, but various colors and regional tying techniques are also embraced.

If you are unsure how to tie a tie, seek guidance from a knowledgeable family member or opt for a pre-tied option to ensure a secure and appropriate fit.

Add a jacket only when needed

A Nehru jacket can add definition to a simple kurta and is perfect for evening occasions or formal pictures, but may be too warm in hot weather.

Being comfortable is essential for dressing up during holidays. Opt for breathable fabrics and shoes that are comfortable for walking.

Coordinate without becoming identical

When taking family photos, it's common to choose coordinating colors. Instead of matching perfectly, consider using a complementary palette such as saffron and cream, green and gold, or maroon and beige.

This maintains a cohesive look while still allowing individuals to express their personal style.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What is a pheta?

The pheta is a customary turban from Maharashtra that is worn during festive, ceremonial, and cultural events.

Does a Gudi Padwa outfit require a dhoti?

Wearing a kurta with pajama, churidar, or trousers can add festivity to your outfit, while a dhoti offers a more traditional choice.

Can I use a pre-tied pheta?

Certainly. It is convenient if you lack a proficient individual to tie the customary method.
