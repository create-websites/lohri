The Evolution of the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade

Follow the evolution of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade from its humble beginnings as a 1924 employee march to its iconic giant balloons, dazzling performances, and status as a television staple.

A holiday parade begins in 1924

Macy's Thanksgiving parade, which dates back to 1924, featured employees in costumes leading animals from Central Park Zoo through Manhattan to the Herald Square store. The purpose of the event was to provide festive entertainment and kick off the holiday shopping season.

The parade became an annual tradition, evolving its visual language alongside the city. Live animal displays were replaced by elaborate spectacles such as floats, marching bands, celebrity appearances, and the iconic giant character balloons.

How the balloons became icons

The parade was easily spotted from blocks away and on television screens nationwide thanks to the oversized balloons. The design of the balloons required expertise in illustration, fabrication, inflation tests, team handling, and weather preparation.

The balloons made the parade flexible to current trends in pop culture. Characters could evolve with each new generation, yet the tradition of watching gigantic figures glide through the streets of Manhattan remained unchanged.

Broadway, television and national reach

With the growth of broadcasting, the parade evolved into more than just a local New York event. Spectators both at home and along the route were treated to musical performances, marching bands, dance groups, and theatrical segments.

The parade's longevity is due to its dual nature: it serves as both a physical community event and a televised spectacle. Families can witness it in person on the street, from the comfort of their living rooms, or through online videos, but the essence remains a shared holiday celebration.

Planning a parade-centered Thanksgiving morning

If watching the parade is a family tradition, plan breakfast and meal prep around the broadcast instead of trying to cook everything at once. Keep kids' activities easy and be aware that the televised schedule and viewing options can vary each year - check the official details closer to the holiday.

Frequently asked questions

When did the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade begin?

Macy’s dates the first parade to 1924.

Were giant balloons part of the very first parade?

Early parades featured live animals, while giant character balloons became a prominent element in later years.
