Vijayadashami in Bengal: Sindoor Khela, Bijoya and Immersion

Discover the vibrant Vijayadashami traditions of Bengal, from the colorful Sindoor Khela and heartfelt Bijoya greetings to the emotional farewell of Maa Durga following the Durga Puja festivities.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

In Bengal, Vijayadashami represents both triumph and parting, with the departure of Maa Durga setting a poignant tone and the anticipation of her return the following year.

The final day of Durga Puja

Following days of worship and community festivities, Vijayadashami signifies the end of Durga Puja. With gratitude and emotion, devotees prepare for the Goddess's immersion through public processions.

The day combines sacred closure with social renewal.

Sindoor Khela

Sindoor Khela is a traditional ceremony where married women apply vermilion to the Goddess and each other in certain communities. Recent practices show a range of participation and a growing inclusivity in the celebration's interpretation.

The public facade is cheerful, yet the tradition is part of a broader series of goodbye customs.

Bijoya Sammelani and greetings

Following the conclusion of the formal puja, individuals exchange Bijoya greetings, visit loved ones, share sweets, and strengthen social connections. Bijoya Sammelani gatherings may extend beyond the immersion period.

The conclusion of a religious festival marks the commencement of a social season focused on reconciliation and connection.

Immersion and impermanence

The emotional intensity of immersing Durga images lies in the deliberate act of returning something beautiful and meticulously crafted back to the water. This ritual serves as a lesson in impermanence, yet also maintains continuity with the anticipation of their return the following year.

Victory, departure and renewal all coexist in the same moment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What is Sindoor Khela?

In certain Bengali Durga Puja traditions, vermilion is utilized as part of a Vijayadashami ritual.

What does Bijoya mean socially?

After Durga Puja, a time of reunions, visits, treats, and renewed bonds begins.

Why is immersion emotional?

As Maa Durga departs, she leaves behind the promise of her return in the next annual cycle.
