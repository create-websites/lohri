Leftover Easter Eggs: Beyond Deviled Eggs

Transform chilled hard-boiled eggs into salads, savory pastries, grain bowls, and quick breakfasts with ease.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🌼 EasterCulinary & Baking Trends

Start with safe leftovers

Use only eggs that have been safely handled and refrigerated. Eggs that have been stored outdoors, in warm rooms, or in questionable hiding spots should not be used for cooking.

Make a flexible egg salad base

Cut eggs into small pieces and mix with a touch of mayo or yogurt, mustard, herbs, and crunchy veggies. Enjoy in sandwiches, lettuce wraps, wraps, or on toast to keep things interesting. Instead of making a big batch that gets boring, mix it up.

Add eggs to savory meals

Quartered eggs are a versatile addition to grain bowls, composed salads, curries, and potato dishes. Chopped eggs can enhance hand pies, stuffed pastries, or breakfast casseroles.

Use the yolks creatively

You can incorporate extra yolks into sauces and dressings, and chop whites into salads. Planning multiple meals helps keep leftovers exciting and minimizes waste.

Frequently asked questions

Can I eat eggs used in an outdoor Easter hunt?

If they were stored properly and maintained in a clean, uncracked condition within safe time and temperature parameters.

What is an easy alternative to deviled eggs?

Egg salad, grain bowls, and savory breakfast dishes are all easy ways to utilize multiple eggs in one go.
