10 Rangoli Ideas for Gudi Padwa

Celebrate the Maharashtrian New Year with easy rangoli designs influenced by traditional symbols like the Gudi, lotus flowers, mango leaves, sunbursts, kalash shapes, and geometric patterns.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

Gudi Padwa comes alive with clothing and home décor that bring a festive feel in an accessible and easy-to-execute way.

Why rangoli belongs at the entrance

Rangoli adorns the entrance as a symbol of hospitality. During Gudi Padwa, it links the decorated doorway, toran, elevated Gudi, and incoming visitors. Even a simple pattern can add a sense of ceremony to the entryway.

Select a scale that suits your doorway size and the level of foot traffic. It is preferable to have a compact design that stays in place rather than a large one that requires everyone to step over it.

1–3: Gudi, lotus and kalash motifs

  1. Mini Gudi: Create a tall banner design featuring foliage and a petite vessel at the top.
  2. Eight-petal lotus: utilize a basic flower at the center with saffron and white petals in alternating pattern.
  3. Kalash geometry: merge the silhouette of a pot with the intricate design of mango leaves and a radiating edge.

4–6: Sunburst, mango leaves and peacock accents

  1. Sunburst: create concentric circles with rays to suggest a bright beginning.
  2. Mango-leaf border: repeat green leaf shapes around a square or semicircle.
  3. Peacock corner motif: Instead of using a detailed full bird, opt for a stylized peacock on the edge.

7–10: Dots, florals and modern minimal patterns

  1. Dot-grid rangoli: build a symmetrical pattern from a small grid.
  2. Marigold petal rangoli: use real flower petals for a low-waste design.
  3. White line rangoli: keep the palette minimal and let geometry do the work.
  4. Family initials + Gudi border: personalize a modern design while keeping a festival symbol.

Color palette ideas

Saffron, yellow, green, white, and deep red complement Gudi Padwa celebrations. To create a cohesive entrance, match the dominant color of your Gudi cloth in the rangoli design.

Families seeking alternatives to synthetic color powder may consider using natural flower petals and reusable rangoli stencils.

Make it child-friendly

Offer children a designated area within the design instead of the entire canvas. They can choose to fill in petals, position flower heads, or utilize a stencil. This approach results in a neater outcome while allowing them to feel a sense of ownership.

It is important to always store powders in a safe place where they are out of reach of young children and pets, especially when the ingredients are not clearly labeled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Which colors suit a Gudi Padwa rangoli?

Saffron, yellow, green, white, and red harmonize effortlessly with the festival's decor.

What is the easiest rangoli for beginners?

A simple lotus, sunburst, or dot-grid design is typically less challenging than a large, intricate freehand scene.

Can flowers replace rangoli powder?

Indeed, Marigold and various other petals can be used to craft a vibrant entrance design that minimizes waste.
