A Complete Plant-Based Thanksgiving Feast

Create a delightful vegetarian or vegan Thanksgiving spread featuring flavorful main dishes, delectable sides, indulgent gravies, and festive desserts that take center stage, not a backseat.

Design around a real centerpiece

For a successful plant-based Thanksgiving, focus on main dishes with structure, aroma, and visual appeal like a mushroom-and-lentil Wellington, stuffed squash, savory nut roast, vegetable pot pie, or layered filo pie instead of relying solely on side dishes to make the meal complete.

Build depth with umami and browning

Utilize mushrooms, caramelized onions, miso, toasted nuts, roasted garlic, and deeply browned vegetables to infuse your dishes with the rich flavors typically found in slow-cooked meat recipes. Incorporate them into gravies, stuffings, and main courses for a cohesive and flavorful dining experience.

Classic sides are easy to adapt

Mashed potatoes can be made with olive oil, plant butter, and unsweetened plant milk. Stuffing can be prepared using vegetable stock. Green bean casserole can be made with dairy-free mushroom sauce. Many recipes for cranberry sauce are naturally plant-based.

Please be sure to scrutinize packaged ingredients for any animal-derived components, such as stocks, pie crusts, marshmallows, and flavorings.

Dessert should still feel like Thanksgiving

Plant-based custard ingredients can be used to make pumpkin pie, while apple crisp, fruit galettes, and pecan-style tarts are also easily adaptable. Complete the meal with coffee, tea, and a nonalcoholic sparkling drink to ensure the same festive feel as a traditional feast.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good vegan centerpiece?

Stuffed squash, mushroom-lentil Wellington, vegetable pot pie, and savory nut roasts all make for a hearty main dish.

Do I need separate dishes for non-vegan guests?

A plant-based menu that is thoughtfully designed can cater to all diners. Keep dairy or meat additions separate instead of incorporating them into every dish.
