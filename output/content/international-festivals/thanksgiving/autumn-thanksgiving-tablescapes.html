Autumn Thanksgiving Tablescapes on a Budget

Design a stylish Thanksgiving table using gourds, foliage, pinecones, candles, layered linens, name cards, and reusable decor in place of costly centerpieces.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHosting, Prep & Decor

Start with texture, not shopping

Create a stunning table setting without breaking the bank by starting with your existing collection of neutral plates, basic glassware, cloth napkins, and a table runner. Incorporate elements of autumn such as leaves, pinecones, branches, small pumpkins, or fresh seasonal fruit for added texture and charm.

Keep the centerpiece low

Guests should have visibility of each other by utilizing small clusters of gourds, bud vases, low greenery, or candles instead of a single tall arrangement. If opting for candles, ensure flames are kept a safe distance from dry leaves, paper, and children.

Use napkins and place cards as color

Using a folded cloth napkin, ribbon, or handwritten name card can create a stronger visual impact than adding more objects. By repeating two or three colors throughout the table, you can make simple items feel purposeful.

Design for food service

Prior to arranging the table, determine the placement of serving dishes. For family-style dinners, ensure there are ample empty spaces. For buffet-style dinners, focus on decorative elements as the platters will be located elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest inexpensive centerpiece?

An arrangement of small pumpkins or gourds, along with trimmed greenery and a couple of short candles, is a straightforward, versatile, and easily adjustable display.

Should I decorate with real leaves?

While dry leaves should be kept away from open flames and food surfaces, artificial or fresh greenery can be a safer option for indoor use.
