Why Football Became a Thanksgiving Tradition

Trace the evolution of Thanksgiving football from college games in the late 1800s to its inclusion in the holiday traditions of Detroit, Dallas, and the NFL schedule.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHistory, Heritage & Perspectives

College football helped set the pattern

Football and Thanksgiving were associated long before the NFL as we know it today. College games in the late 1800s often scheduled rivalry matchups on the holiday, taking advantage of the break from regular routines for students and spectators.

The connection was logical: as football and Thanksgiving grew in popularity as public traditions, newspaper coverage transformed key games into communal regional spectacles.

The NFL builds an annual tradition

Professional football has a long history of celebrating Thanksgiving, with the NFL officially recognizing Thanksgiving games dating back to 1920. Detroit began hosting a Thanksgiving game in 1934, and the tradition has continued annually since 1945. Dallas became a regular Thanksgiving host in 1966.

In 2006, a Thanksgiving night game was introduced, extending the schedule beyond the traditional Detroit and Dallas games and creating a day-long media tradition for countless households.

Why the tradition stuck

Thanksgiving football is a perfect fit for the day's flow, providing a shared backdrop for cooking, socializing, and unwinding post-feast. It offers a simple communal activity for all generations, even amidst varying guest interests.

Not all households follow the same tradition. While some may choose to skip sports altogether, others make the entire afternoon revolve around kickoff. Thanksgiving is flexible enough to accommodate both approaches.

Make football serve the gathering—not dominate it

To accommodate guests with different preferences, create separate areas for watching games, chatting, and playing games. Designate a specific meal time for everyone to come together, keeping noise levels down and avoiding distractions like a TV at the dining table to ensure inclusivity.

Frequently asked questions

When did NFL Thanksgiving games begin?

The NFL started recording Thanksgiving games during its inaugural season in 1920.

Why are the Lions and Cowboys associated with Thanksgiving?

Detroit started hosting Thanksgiving games regularly in the 1930s and has done so every year since 1945, while Dallas began its own tradition in 1966.
