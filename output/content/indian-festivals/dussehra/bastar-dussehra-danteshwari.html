Bastar Dussehra: The 75-Day Festival of Goddess Danteshwari

Discover Bastar Dussehra, a 75-day tribal festival dedicated to Goddess Danteshwari, with a focus on community involvement and traditional regional rituals.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

Bastar Dussehra defies the belief that all Dussehras are centered around Rama, Ravana, and burning effigies, instead focusing on Goddess Danteshwari and the rich ritual traditions of Bastar.

India’s unusually long Dussehra cycle

The editorial plan outlines Bastar Dussehra as a 75-day festival, marking it as one of the lengthiest celebrations linked with Dussehra. The rituals are spread out over various stages instead of being condensed into a single evening.

The prolonged timeframe illustrates a sacred calendar specific to the region, complete with its unique customs and duties to the community.

Goddess Danteshwari at the center

In contrast to the Rama-Ravana narrative of North India, Bastar Dussehra honors Goddess Danteshwari and is influenced by local goddess traditions, tribal involvement, and the historical customs of the Bastar region.

Visitors must refrain from viewing it as a mere variation of Ravana Dahan.

Community labor and ritual participation

Many different aspects contribute to the success of large public festivals, including processional work, ritual roles, preparation, music, crafts, and collective coordination. The celebration in Bastar is particularly significant in demonstrating how community involvement can become a sacred practice.

The event demonstrates the preservation of regional identity through recurrent public ceremonies.

How to approach as a visitor

It is important for travelers to prioritize cultural respect, seek local guidance, and obtain consent when taking photographs. Ritual spaces should not be treated as staged performances, even when they are visited by outsiders.

An attentive visit prioritizes listening and understanding over gathering souvenirs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Is Bastar Dussehra about Ravana Dahan?

The festival revolves around the worship of Goddess Danteshwari and the celebration of Bastar's local customs.

Why is it described as 75 days?

The editorial brief describes it as a lengthy ritual cycle lasting about 75 days.

Where is it celebrated?

It is associated with the Bastar region of Chhattisgarh.
