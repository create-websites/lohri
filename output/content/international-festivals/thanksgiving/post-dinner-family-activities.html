Post-Dinner Thanksgiving Activities Beyond TV

Continue the Thanksgiving festivities post-dinner with activities like walks, board games, memory-sharing, crafts, neighborhood fun, and relaxed traditions suitable for everyone.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingGratitude, Wellness & Traditions

Take the energy outside

Taking a brief stroll can help reenergize everyone after a big meal and provide kids with space to stretch their legs. While some areas host Turkey Trots in the morning, others opt for a leisurely walk around the block post-dinner.

Choose games with low barriers

Choose games that are easy to pick up and suitable for players of all ages. Charades, drawing games, trivia, cards, and cooperative board games tend to be more enjoyable than complex games that may alienate casual players.

Create a family archive

Capture a grandparent sharing a cherished family tale, browse through old photographs, recreate a special moment in history, or have each individual recall a favorite memory from the past year. These activities create lasting memories that go beyond just one evening.

Leave room for quiet

Not all individuals seek structured activities. Consider setting up a puzzle table, reading nook, or crafting space where guests can come and go as they please. A fulfilling vacation does not hinge on constant planned entertainment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good activity for mixed ages?

Activities such as taking a stroll, playing charades, simple card games, sharing photos, and solving puzzles together can be enjoyable for people of all ages

Should every minute be planned?

It's generally better to have a few optional activities rather than a strict schedule following a big meal.
