Giving Back Locally During Thanksgiving

Show support for food banks, shelters, and delivery programs during Thanksgiving by inquiring about their specific needs and maintaining a balance between holiday generosity and ongoing assistance.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingGratitude, Wellness & Traditions

Ask organizations what is actually useful

Food banks and shelters may have certain requirements, space constraints, and volunteer guidelines. Prior to planning a collection, be sure to review the organization's wish list. Cash donations can be more cost-effective than individual purchases, as organizations can buy in bulk.

Think beyond canned-food drives

Different communities may have varying needs such as hygiene products, transportation assistance, meal delivery, winter clothing, gift cards, translation services, or ongoing financial support, all of which can be valuable forms of assistance.

Volunteer with humility

Holiday volunteer opportunities can fill up fast. If Thanksgiving Day is already booked, think about volunteering on the days before or after, or commit to a regular shift later in the winter. Please follow the guidance of staff and refrain from taking photos of recipients unless given explicit permission.

Make service part of family tradition

Kids can assist with organizing pantry-safe donations, creating encouraging cards for organizations in need, or selecting a cause to support as a family. The aim is to cultivate a culture of community involvement beyond just one holiday.

Frequently asked questions

Is donating money better than donating food?

Food banks can effectively stretch funds, but requirements differ. Before making a decision, consult the organization's latest guidelines.

What if Thanksgiving volunteer shifts are full?

Volunteer an extra day or provide ongoing support. Organizations require assistance year-round, not just during holidays.
