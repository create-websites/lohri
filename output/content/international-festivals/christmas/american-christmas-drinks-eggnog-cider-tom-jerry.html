American Christmas Drinks: Eggnog, Cider and Tom & Jerry

American holiday drink culture mixes old recipes, winter spices and modern party service—from chilled eggnog to steaming cider and the historic Tom & Jerry.

Eggnog as a holiday staple

Eggnog is a festive drink made with a blend of dairy, eggs, sugar, and nutmeg, often spiked with spirits for adults. Recipes range from cooked custards to raw-egg concoctions.

To ensure food safety for vulnerable guests, either use pasteurized eggs or a cooked recipe and refrigerate dairy-based nog.

Mulled cider for all ages

Hot apple cider infused with cinnamon, cloves, citrus, and various spices can easily be made in large quantities and enjoyed by all, even those who prefer to skip the alcohol. Plus, it fills the air with a delightful aroma as it simmers.

Store spices in a sachet or strain before serving to prevent guests from retrieving whole cloves from their drinks.

The Tom & Jerry cocktail

The Tom & Jerry is a historic warm holiday drink built around a rich batter combined with hot liquid and spirits. It survives as a regional seasonal tradition more than an everyday national drink.

Clearly label the alcoholic drinks and provide a non-alcoholic option to accommodate all guests, rather than attempting to cater to everyone with one pot.

Build a practical holiday drink station

Provide guests with water, a hot nonalcoholic drink, a cold option, and, if appropriate, an adults-only alcoholic choice. Keep garnishes simple to prevent guests from waiting in line to customize their beverages.

Use cups that are safe for heat and ensure hot beverages are not placed near table edges accessible to children.

Context note: Adults of legal drinking age should enjoy alcoholic holiday beverages. Remember to follow safe handling guidelines and refrigerate eggnog made with eggs.

Frequently asked questions

Can eggnog be alcohol-free?

Indeed, alcohol is not required in many commercial and homemade iterations.

What is a Tom & Jerry?

This traditional holiday drink is a warm, historic cocktail made with a luscious batter and hot liquid, often including spirits in the adult versions.
