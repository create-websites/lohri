How to Make a Mango Leaf and Marigold Toran

Make a new toran for Gudi Padwa by using mango leaves and marigold flowers in a traditional style, with helpful instructions for securing and maintaining its appearance.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

The celebration of Gudi Padwa comes alive with clothing and home décor, showcasing accessible styling and decorations that are both festive and easy to create.

Why the doorway matters

Gudi Padwa festivities kick off at home, starting with cleaning, decorating, creating rangoli, and raising the Gudi. The entrance is adorned with a toran, signaling to all that the household is celebrating a special and auspicious occasion.

Mango leaves and marigolds are favored for their vibrant colors and festive shapes that complement the Gudi beautifully.

Materials

Collect new mango leaves, marigold blooms, sturdy cotton thread or twine, a needle suitable for making garlands if needed, and hooks or ties that won't harm the entrance.

If you can't find fresh mango leaves, opt for safe, sturdy, and culturally appropriate local leaves instead of plastic imitations.

Prepare the leaves and flowers

Carefully remove any damaged leaves and gently wipe the remaining ones. Arrange the leaves by size to create a balanced toran. Select sturdy marigold blooms that will stay intact for hours.

Store materials in a cool, shaded area prior to assembly to maintain freshness.

Two easy assembly styles

  • Alternating style: connect clusters of leaves with a marigold in between each one using either ties or stitches.
  • Garland style: Start by creating a marigold garland, then evenly space mango leaves along the top and secure them in place.

No matter which method you select, be sure to leave sufficient thread on each end for hanging securely.

Hang it safely

Secure the toran with sturdy hooks or ties at a height that prevents tall guests from touching it, and be cautious of placing it near open flames.

To prevent petals from accumulating and creating slippery conditions, make sure to sweep them regularly to keep the entrance clean.

A reusable modern version

Create a fabric toran with marigold-colored flowers for apartments or areas with limited access to fresh materials. Ensure to store it properly for annual reuse.

A handmade toran, designed for reuse, can transform into a cherished family keepsake, holding onto its traditional colors and shapes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

How long will a fresh toran last?

The freshness of leaves and flowers is prolonged when they are kept near the festival, as it is influenced by the levels of heat and humidity.

Can I make a toran without mango leaves?

If you cannot find mango leaves, opt for a considerate local substitute or a reusable cloth option instead of using unsafe or inappropriate plants.

Where should the toran be placed?

Traditionally, it is hung over the main entrance or another prominent doorway of the home.
