Why Vijayadashami matters

One festival, many forms of victory

Dussehra can signify Rama triumphing over Ravana, Durga conquering Mahishasura, the start of learning with Vidyarambham, the reverence of tools with Shastra or Ayudha Puja, or a regional festival highlighting local deities and traditions.
