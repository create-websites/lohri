Why Durga Puja matters

A festival where worship becomes a citywide work of art

During Durga Puja, sacred rituals, temporary structures, idol crafting, music, cuisine, fashion, and community involvement all converge. In Bengal, the Goddess is honored as both a powerful cosmic entity and a beloved daughter coming back to her roots.

This hub categorizes 20 guides into four groups, allowing readers to navigate through ritual significance, crafting techniques, community involvement, and practical Pujo advice.
