Eco-Friendly Dussehra: Ravan Dahan and Low-Waste Ideas

Consider implementing sustainable practices for Dussehra such as eco-friendly decorations, safer effigies, reusable materials, community clean-ups, and eco-conscious alternatives to traditional celebrations to reduce waste and pollution.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

The concept of good triumphing over harmful urges is clearer when the event minimizes unnecessary waste, smoke, and litter.

Start with the largest impacts

Begin by prioritizing the main sources of waste: disposable plastic decorations, large non-recyclable effigies, litter made of mixed materials, excess food waste, and disposable serving ware that is not needed.

A couple of major changes are more significant than many small "green" actions.

Rethink Ravan Dahan materials

When planning community events, prioritize using natural or easily recyclable materials for smaller-scale effigies and designs to minimize toxic paints, plastics, and synthetic fillers. Always prioritize fire safety regulations and local rules.

In places where burning is limited or unsuitable, symbolic light, projection, theater, or reusable installations can convey the message without replicating the exact ceremonial practice.

Decor and food with a second life

Utilize fabric torans, eco-friendly lights, fresh flowers in moderation, and compostable rangoli materials. Carefully portion food and set up a safe and legal process for sharing or donating excess.

The top holiday decoration is typically the one that can be stored and reused in the following year.

Make clean-up part of the event

Before the celebration starts, make sure waste stations, volunteers, and post-event clean-up are assigned. Clearly labeled compost, recycling, and landfill bins are more effective than assuming guests will figure it out on their own.

Sustainability is effective when integrated into logistics, not tacked on as a mere slogan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Can Ravana Dahan be made eco-friendly?

Reducing size, avoiding toxic materials, and adhering to local fire and pollution regulations can help make it more environmentally friendly.

What is the easiest household change?

Replace disposable decor and serving items with reusable alternatives.

What if burning is restricted locally?

Choose a symbolic non-burning alternative and follow local regulations.
