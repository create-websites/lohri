Why Shraddh matters

A ritual language for memory, duty and gratitude

Shraddh offers Hindu families a systematic approach to honoring ancestors through rituals such as offering water, food, charity, prayers, and sharing family memories. Pitru Paksha designates a specific lunar period for this commemoration.
