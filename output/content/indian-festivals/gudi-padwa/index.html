Why Gudi Padwa matters

A New Year raised like a banner

Celebrated on the first day of Chaitra month, Gudi Padwa signifies the start of the Maharashtrian New Year, blending tradition, mythology, family customs, delicious seasonal dishes, community gatherings, and the iconic sight of the Gudi flag displayed prominently outside homes.

This hub categorizes 20 practical and cultural guides into five groups, allowing you to transition from understanding the stories to celebrating them: start by learning the stories, then delve into the rituals, prepare the meal, adorn yourself and your home, and finally, discover sustainable and diaspora variations.
