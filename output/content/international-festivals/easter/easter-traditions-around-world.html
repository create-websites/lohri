Easter Traditions Around the World

Discover various Easter traditions across the globe, from Bermuda's kite flying to Florence's Scoppio del Carro, and other local spring festivities.

Bermuda: kites in the Good Friday sky

In Bermuda, vibrant handmade kites are strongly linked to Good Friday. Families come together outdoors, and the unique appearance and sound of the kites set the day apart from Easter festivities in other places.

Florence: Scoppio del Carro

Florence's renowned Scoppio del Carro, known as the "Explosion of the Cart," features a traditional cart, parade, and dazzling fireworks display near the cathedral. This event holds significance in both local civic history and Easter traditions.

Processions, meals and local landscapes

In Europe, Latin America, Africa, the Middle East, and Asia, Easter celebrations may consist of dawn worship, public Passion plays, processions, decorated eggs, special breads, or large family meals. The holiday's traditions are influenced by climate and local ingredients, in addition to religious beliefs.

Avoid turning traditions into curiosities

Ensure to understand the context and acknowledge the community when adopting ideas from a different culture. A comprehensive Easter guide should emphasize diversity while respecting the authenticity of each tradition instead of merely listing unusual customs.

Frequently asked questions

Why are kites associated with Easter in Bermuda?

Kite flying on Good Friday has become a unique Bermudian tradition and is now a significant part of the holiday weekend.

What is Scoppio del Carro?

Florence's Easter "Explosion of the Cart" is a traditional parade and fireworks display held in the vicinity of the cathedral.
