Tarpan Explained: Water, Black Sesame and the Practice of Remembrance

Gain insight into the significance of Tarpan, the use of water and black sesame, and an appropriate approach to making ancestral offerings during Pitru Paksha.

Shraddh Hub
Why this guide matters

Tarpan is a sacred ritual involving water, remembrance, and invocation, commonly practiced during Pitru Paksha to honor ancestors. It may include offerings of black sesame seeds, specific gestures, and mantras. While the exact procedure may vary, a reliable guide can provide insight into the ritual's significance and direct families to their own traditions for specific details.

Why water is central

Water is a fundamental element that is universally nourishing and readily available. In ancient ceremonies, it transforms into a conduit of remembrance: briefly grasped before being set free.

The act of both grasping and releasing imbues Tarpan with a potent symbolical essence, even without delving into its theological significance.

Why black sesame is used

Black sesame, also known as til, is commonly used in ancestral rituals, with traditional beliefs linking it to purity, protection, and its appropriateness for Pitru karma.

Symbolism should not be dismissed as mere superstition; instead, ritual materials function within a broader framework of intention, mantra, timing, and ancestral traditions.

A respectful basic framework

An easy way to observe may involve taking a bath, putting on fresh clothes, creating a peaceful space, honoring ancestors by name, giving water with sesame, and ending with a prayer or act of kindness.

Families looking for precise formal rituals should consult their lineage or priest as hand positions, direction, mantra, and sequence may vary.

What matters when living abroad

Diaspora families without access to traditional religious sites can still honor their ancestors at home if they follow local environmental regulations and dispose of offerings properly.

The essential principle is sincerity without turning adaptation into anxiety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Must Tarpan be performed beside a river?

Rivers hold sacred significance, yet numerous families follow their traditions by making water offerings in their homes.

Is black sesame compulsory?

Many ancestral ceremonies follow tradition, although the specific materials used may differ.

Can I perform a simple Tarpan without a priest?

Some families opt for a more simplified way of remembering their ancestors at home, while seeking trusted guidance for formal rituals and lineage-specific practices.
