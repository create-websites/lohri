Ramlila of Varanasi: Open-Air Theatre and Living Tradition

Explore the open-air Ramlila customs of Varanasi, their UNESCO-acknowledged historical significance, and the communal experience that draws audiences into the narrative.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

Ramlila goes beyond just a stage performance. In enduring customs, entire communities and public areas can be transformed into the story's setting.

The Ramayana performed in public space

Ramlila depicts the Ramayana across several evenings, typically concluding during Dussehra. In Varanasi, renowned outdoor performances take place in streets, open spaces, and various locations instead of a traditional theater setting.

As the action unfolds, the audience has the opportunity to physically engage and transform from mere spectators to active participants in a pilgrimage-like experience

UNESCO-recognized heritage context

The editorial brief highlights the UNESCO-recognized Ramlila traditions, showcasing it as a vibrant performance tradition encompassing oral recitation, acting, music, costume, community organization, and inherited practice.

The cultural significance is rooted in its passing down through generations, not just in the ultimate presentation.

Why place matters

As a scene plays out in a real public setting, the geography becomes intertwined with the storytelling. Different locations represent different settings like Ayodhya, a forest, or a battlefield, and the movement between them adds a physical dimension to the narrative.

Long-form Ramlila can create an immersive experience even without the use of modern theatrical technology.

Watching respectfully

Visitors can anticipate a blend of solemnity and entertainment at the event. Be mindful of procession etiquette, adhere to photography guidelines, and respect that for many attendees, the event is a sacred experience.

To truly grasp it, one must watch more than just the intense moments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Is Ramlila only performed on Dussehra day?

Numerous traditions span over multiple days or weeks and culminate during Dussehra.

Why is the Varanasi tradition distinctive?

Outdoor, multi-site performances incorporate the urban scenery into the storyline.

Is Ramlila theatre or worship?

Its functionality can vary depending on the community and participants involved.
