A Plant-Based Easter Brunch Everyone Can Enjoy

Create a bountiful vegan Easter spread featuring savory entrees, vibrant spring accompaniments, and decadent desserts that rival traditional options.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🌼 EasterCulinary & Baking Trends

Lead with real dishes, not replacements

A successful plant-based brunch doesn't have to replicate traditional egg and dairy dishes. Instead, focus on mushroom tarts, roasted asparagus, herbed potatoes, grain salads, fruit, and breads that stand out on their own.

Use a centerpiece people can slice and share

Experience the deliciousness of a vegetable galette, lentil-mushroom loaf, savory strata with plant-based custard, or a lemon and herb roasted cauliflower centerpiece. Sharing a main dish adds a festive touch to the table.

Make baking easier with a few reliable swaps

Opt for plant milks, neutral oil, vegan butter, and flax or commercial egg replacers when making vegan brunch bakes, and stick to recipes specifically designed to be vegan to avoid last-minute conversions for delicate dishes like custards or soufflés.

Label the buffet clearly

If guests have allergies and dietary preferences, label dishes and use separate serving utensils. Just because something is "plant-based" doesn't mean it's free of nuts, gluten, or soy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good vegan Easter brunch centerpiece?

A plant-based strata, mushroom-lentil loaf, or vegetable galette can serve as the centerpiece of the meal.

Should I try to veganize every traditional dish?

It's possible that a menu focused on naturally plant-based dishes can result in a more harmonious flavor profile.
