Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree: History and Design

The Rockefeller Center tree is a massive engineering feat and a beloved public tradition, turning a single towering spruce into an iconic holiday symbol recognized nationwide.

How the tradition began

The tradition of the Rockefeller Center tree can be traced back to the early 1930s, when workers put up a Christmas tree at the construction site. This led to an official annual lighting ceremony that has since become a significant event in New York City.

As time passed, the tree became more than just a local decoration, transforming into a national holiday symbol through broadcast coverage, skating-rink imagery, and tourism.

How a tree is selected

The selected tree is usually a massive Norway spruce sourced from the New York area, chosen based on its visual appearance, structural integrity, ease of removal, and the challenges of transporting such a tall tree on public roads.

When making the final decision, it's not only height that matters; symmetry, branch density, and safe movement are equally important.

Transport, rigging and lights

Transporting and setting up a tree of this size involves using cranes, wrapping for protection, planning the route, and coordinating a team. After securing the tree, workers then add the star topper and install plenty of lighting.

Visitors may not notice the engineering behind it, but it is what allows the display to be safely maintained in a busy urban plaza for weeks.

Visiting without the stress

For a more peaceful experience, consider visiting earlier in the day or during the off-peak season. Be prepared for crowd management measures during busy evenings and opt for public transportation whenever feasible.

Instead of attempting to visit every Midtown attraction in one short trip, consider exploring the tree along with the nearby windows, churches, Bryant Park, or Fifth Avenue.

Context note: Please check the official Rockefeller Center information for updates on lighting schedules, viewing guidelines, and security measures that may vary each season before your visit.

Frequently asked questions

When did the Rockefeller Center tree tradition begin?

It is commonly believed to have originated in the early 1930s, followed shortly by the development of the annual lighting tradition.

What kind of tree is usually used?

A large Norway spruce is the most common choice.
